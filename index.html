THE HEADLINE

Six KPIs, before and after

Every KPI from our KPI deck moves in the right direction, except profit per order before refunds, which stays the same because prices, menu and channel mix were deliberately left unchanged.

CHAPTER 1

Fewer wrong orders, especially at Flame and Bun

The packing Accuracy Checker makes shift leads tick every item before an order leaves. Flame and Bun, the weakest brand, gains the most.

CHAPTER 2

The rush hour no longer breaks the kitchen

Peak hours were where accuracy fell and prep times ran long. With the order hub and staffing alert, both gaps narrow.

CHAPTER 3

Same staff hours, moved to where the orders are

The Staffing Alert flags any hour above 2 orders waiting per staff member. Dataset 2 moves one person from 10:00, 17:00 and 21:00 into 11:00, 13:00 and 20:00, so the total stays at 58 staff hours a day, while faster tickets mean fewer orders pile up.

CHAPTER 4

Customers notice

A wrong order costs about 1.7 stars. Fewer wrong orders and shorter delays lift every brand's average rating.

CHAPTER 5

Refunds are cut in half

Refunds only happen on wrong orders, so catching mistakes before they leave the kitchen shows up directly as money kept.

CHAPTER 6

More profit kept per order

Profit after refunds is the order's estimated profit minus any refunded amount. Phone/text orders still earn the most because they pay no commission, which the Profit Tracker makes visible to the owner.
