THE HEADLINE

Six KPIs, before and after

All six KPIs from our KPI deck move in the right direction. Profit per order before refunds stays about the same ($9.24 vs $9.22) because prices, menu and channel mix were deliberately left unchanged.

TIMELINE

Month by month: 2025 before, 2026 after

Each KPI for all 24 months. The dashed line marks Jan 1, 2026, when the app goes live; the flat dashed lines are each year's average. Choose a KPI to see where the change happens.

CHAPTER 1

Fewer wrong orders, especially at Flame and Bun

The Packing Check makes staff mark every item Correct or Wrong before an order leaves. Flame and Bun, the weakest brand, gains the most.

CHAPTER 2

The rush hour no longer breaks the kitchen

Peak hours were where accuracy fell and prep times ran long. With the order hub and staffing alert, both gaps narrow.

CHAPTER 3

Same staff hours, moved to where the orders are

The Staffing Alert flags any hour above 2 orders waiting per staff member. Dataset 2 moves one person from 10:00, 17:00 and 21:00 into 11:00, 13:00 and 20:00, so the total stays at 58 staff hours a day, while faster tickets mean fewer orders pile up.

CHAPTER 4

Customers notice

A wrong order costs about 1.7 stars. Fewer wrong orders and shorter delays lift every brand's average rating.

CHAPTER 5

Refunds are cut in half

Refunds only happen on wrong orders, so catching mistakes before they leave the kitchen shows up directly as money kept.

CHAPTER 6

More profit kept per order

Profit after refunds is the order's estimated profit minus any refunded amount. Phone/text orders still earn the most because they pay no commission, which the Profit Tracker makes visible to the owner.

METHOD

How Dataset 2 was built

Dataset 2 covers Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2026, the year right after Dataset 1. Each 2026 order is drawn at random from the 2025 orders and placed on the same calendar day, so unchanged variables keep Dataset 1's averages and ups and downs. Only the variables below were then changed, each by a published amount and with random variation around the new average.
